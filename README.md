# Mama Minds

**Postpartum mental health support — for after birth, when the check-ins stop.**

---

During pregnancy, care is close. Appointments every few weeks. A midwife or nurse asking how you're doing at every visit. People around you paying attention to how you look, how you're eating, how you're sleeping.

Then you give birth, and within a few days, that attention shifts. It shifts to the baby — which makes sense, of course it does — but it also just... drops off. The follow-ups thin out. Family goes back to their own lives. People ask "how's the baby?" more often than they ask how you are.

And that's exactly when postpartum depression most commonly surfaces. Not during pregnancy. After. In the weeks and months when the monitoring stops and the world assumes you're fine.

Mama Minds exists to fill that gap. A quiet, private space to check in on how you're actually doing — every day if you want, or just when you need it.

---

## What it does

- **EPDS Assessment** — the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale, a clinically validated 10-question check-in that helps you understand your wellbeing, with guidance based on your results
- **Daily mood tracking** — quick check-ins to notice how you're feeling over time, without judgement
- **Resources** — offline articles on understanding postpartum depression, coping strategies, family support, helplines, and how to talk to a healthcare provider — available even without internet
- **Support network alerts** — a fast way to reach a healthcare worker or loved one when you need backup
- **Works offline** — install it on your home screen once, and it stays with you without needing a connection

## How to use it

Visit: **https://cindyleahphoto-ctrl.github.io/Mama-Minds-PPD/**

To install on your phone for offline access:
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → tap the Share icon → "Add to Home Screen"

Once installed, it works like any other app — no internet required after the first open.

---

## Privacy

All your data (check-ins, assessment results, profile info) stays on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server or shared with anyone unless you choose to use the alert feature to message a healthcare worker or loved one directly.
